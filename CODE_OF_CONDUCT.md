# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, constructive space for everyone.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical disagreements in good faith.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Please do not raise sensitive reports in a public issue.
